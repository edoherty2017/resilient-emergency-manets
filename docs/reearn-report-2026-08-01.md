## 2. Per-family summaries

**Family 1 — SOS-retry ablation (rotate_lb ± `--sos-retry`).** Retry rescues SOS delivery in every seed: per-seed deltas +3.8/+5.4/+2.5 pp, paired mean +3.9 pp ±1.6 (0.9576 → 0.9964), i.e. ~5–10 lost incidents/yr converted to delayed deliveries — close to the withdrawn "+4.2 points". Airtime cost is +0.01% relative, confirming "negligible airtime". The price is entirely in the tail: p50 unchanged at 0.75 s, p95 ~14 s → ~42 s, worst case ~61 s → 25–100 min (7–8 deliveries per seed ride the 5-min retry loop; mean tries 1.00 → 1.67). PDR (0.7751 both arms), deaths (~4,860, 87 unique nodes), and availability (0.9106) are flat. Seed 42 still lost 1 incident with retry on — the retry ceiling is real.

**Family 2 — regional channels (duty_sync ± `--regional-channels`).** The rejection re-earns: channels buy almost nothing and measurably hurt delivery. Contention relief is marginal — offered airtime 0.2347 → 0.2323 (−0.24 pt, far below the old "~3 points"), receiver-busy p95 unchanged, duty misses −5.6%. Against that, PDR drops a perfectly seed-consistent 2.24 pp (0.6876 → 0.6652; −0.0224 in all three seeds), SOS is directionally worse (535/572 vs 539/572 pooled, −0.68 pp, ns; s42 actually favored ON by +1), and mean SOS latency worsens +77 s ±35 (tail-driven; medians identical at 0.75 s). Deaths and availability indistinguishable. The "single shared channel + duty MAC" architecture stands, but the withdrawn 10/178-SOS magnitude (a flood-arm figure) is not re-earned.

**Family 3 — kiosk zero spares (rotate_lb, spares 0 vs 2).** With zero spares, rental availability is 100% in all three seeds — 77,380/77,380 walker-days served, zero starvation (no_stock/unserviceable/unusable all 0), mean checkout SOC 1.00 — identical to the 2-spares arm. Knock-ons are nil despite 38 fewer radios: PDR 0.7751 both arms, deaths 4,863.3 vs 4,862.0 (within seed noise), 87 unique nodes died in both, SOS delivery identical per seed (185/186, 185/186, 200/200). The shuttle-is-binding headline re-earns at this cell; the 2× demand and spares {1,4} cells were not re-run.

**Family 4 — peak demand 4× (renters 8 vs 2).** Graceful-degradation direction re-earns. In the clean matched duty pair (identical 300/900 s cadence, only renters 2→8): PDR actually +1.1 pt (0.6876 → 0.6988), SOS identical (539/572 = 94.2% both), deaths +0.8%, offered airtime 0.235 → 0.430 — ~2.3× headroom, not the withdrawn "3×". Flood at 4× saturates in offered-airtime terms (2.228 vs baseline 0.624), loses 7.5 pt PDR (0.9111 → 0.8363), takes its first SOS losses (100% → 98.6%, 8/572) with ~19× p95 latency inflation (43 → 803 s) — though this pair is cadence-confounded (see caveats). Flood still wins absolute PDR/SOS at 4× by brute redundancy, but with zero margin, 23× the deaths (29,485 vs 1,264) and fleet availability 0.526 vs 0.963. "SOS survived everywhere" does **not** re-earn (no arm at 100%). Kiosk rentals collapse to 0.693 availability (94,913 starved walker-days) at 4× under both policies.

**Family 5 — gateway redundancy (30-d midwinter summit outage).** The single-gateway version re-earns: SOS delivery identical per seed with and without the outage (185/186, 185/186, 200/200 — the one miss also occurs with no outage), p50 latency 0.75 s in all six runs, and total PDR cost is tiny and consistent: paired Δ −0.0017/−0.0017/−0.0016 → −0.00167 ±0.00007 (−0.17 pp on 0.775). The reroute mechanism is visible: summit rx −230k; ridge relays pick up +3k–14k tx each; pinkham_notch_vc (+19.1k rx), gateway_marshfield (+14.3k), highland_center (+6.7k) absorb the traffic at no energy penalty (deaths +2.7 of ~4,865; 0 new dead nodes at the relays). Damage is localized to the summit catchment (−1.4 to −2.3 pp on high-traffic origins; only 6/337 origins lose >2 pp). But the withdrawn claim killed **two** gateways simultaneously — and Pinkham Notch is the largest absorber here — so the dual-gateway wording remains withdrawn pending a dual-outage rerun. "No measurable PDR" should now read "−0.17 pp, measurable but negligible".

**Family 6 — climate robustness across 10 pinned ERA5 years.** The survival headline re-earns in every year, and stronger than the withdrawn ≈7×: energy_aware records 15.4×–26.5× more deaths than duty_sync (mean 20.7×; absolute gap 24,690–27,922, mean 27,013 ±621 across years). Worst duty year (2018, 1,710 deaths — the same worst winter as the original) still beats the best energy_aware year (2018, 26,400) by 15.4×. Year-to-year variance (duty sd ≈235 deaths) is small versus the ~27,000 algorithm effect, and the 2025 slot matches release_v1 canon on deaths (duty 1,255 vs 1,217; ea 29,175 vs 29,161). But the ancillary sub-claims flip sign: duty now pays an explicit delivery cost every year — PDR 0.688 vs 0.843 (−15.5 pp) and SOS 94.6–96.8% vs 99.5–100% — so the old "duty also matches PDR/SOS at 99.4–100%" wording must not be carried forward.

**Family 7 — RL reruns (q_routing / rl_duty).** Both withdrawn RL wins are dead on the corrected engine. q_routing is 4.25 PDR pts **worse** than etx (0.8238 vs 0.8663 ±0.0001), failing even the ±1 pt convergence gate; its surviving result is parity on deaths (29,159 vs 29,165) and SOS (189/189), consistent with the pre-registered expectation that routing cannot fix survival. rl_duty has the fewest deaths (843, −31% vs duty_sync 1,217.4, −51% vs duty_adaptive 1,737.0) and best availability (0.9772) — but at PDR 0.6275, 10.1–12.7 pts below the hand-tuned duty modes, so the pre-registered gate (">15% deaths at equivalent PDR, across seeds) fails on both conditions. The learned policy collapsed to the 2% duty floor in 50/57 visited states (mean duty 0.0352 vs duty_sync 0.0619): it slid down the survival-vs-delivery curve rather than beating it — the docs' pre-labeled "adds nothing over the hand-tuned 5%" reportable negative result.

## 3. Caveats and comparability flags

**Global.**
- All results are simulation only, `claim_status = MODEL_ONLY_uncalibrated`; energy constants remain BENCH-CALIBRATE (open decision G2). No field validation. SOS incident rate is a scenario input, not measured demand.
- Most families run n=3 seeds; CIs (1.96·sd/√3) are crude and understate true uncertainty. SOS-level deltas are generally underpowered at ~190 SOS/yr.
- The re-earn campaign runs at a hotter config than release_v1 (beacon 300 s vs 900, telemetry/route-refresh 900 s vs 3600, energy_step 60 s vs 600 → ~10.0M vs ~3.3M packets). Within-family arm-vs-arm comparisons are valid; **re-earn absolute numbers must not be quoted as release_v1 baselines** (e.g., rotate_lb PDR 0.7751 re-earn vs 0.8089 release_v1, same mode/seed).

**Family-specific.**
- **F1:** original ablation was lb_energy on the superseded engine; re-earn is rotate_lb — "+4.2 vs +3.9 pp" is the same qualitative claim re-earned, not a numeric reproduction. Delivery rate alone hides the latency cliff (losses become 5–100 min deliveries); the +30/+60 s duplicate window is active in both arms.
- **F2:** no collision counters exist in the run JSONs — contention is inferred from offered airtime, receiver-busy occupancy, and duty misses. Only duty_sync±regional was re-run; the withdrawn 10/178 headline came from the flood+channels arm and is not re-earned. High-traffic operating point (~23% offered airtime).
- **F3:** only one cell of the E7 grid re-earned (rotate_lb, normal demand, spares 0 vs 2); 2× demand, spares {1,4}, and stress-time "checkout charge 100%" untested. min checkout SOC = the configured 0.2 floor in both arms, so "100% charge" holds only as the mean. Fleet size differs by construction (419 vs 457 nodes). Hardware failure/theft remains unmodeled.
- **F4:** default renters/route is 2 (not 3 as the task framing said). The flood pair is cadence-confounded — no flood renters=2 run at re-earn cadence exists, so peak_flood was compared against release_v1 fs_flood (900/3600 cadence); the duty pair shows cadence alone costs ~4 pt PDR, so flood's pure-demand degradation is smaller than the −7.5 pt headline (saturation conclusion still safe). "Saturation" is in aggregate **offered** airtime (channel_utilization is a deprecated alias, not physical occupancy; physical receiver-busy p95 at 4× flood is 0.273). Seed sets differ (42–44 vs 42–46; immaterial, PDR CI ≤0.0001). Absolute PDR levels are far below the withdrawn table — only direction re-earned.
- **F5:** scenario is not the withdrawn one — one gateway for 30 days on rotate_lb+retry vs two gateways for 7 days on lb_energy. Pinkham Notch, the largest reroute absorber (+19k rx), was alive here; dual-outage rerun needed for the dual-gateway wording. Run JSONs carry no SOS timestamps, so in-window SOS count (~16 expected fleet-wide if uniform) is unconfirmed. Outage-arm's lower SOS p95 is small-n noise — do not claim a latency improvement. Low-traffic origin deltas (e.g., pinkham_notch_vc −4.2 pp on 36 packets) are noise.
- **F6:** n=1 seed per year per arm — CIs are across years, not seeds. Always-on arm changed (lb_energy → energy_aware), so 7× → ~21× partly reflects the arm swap plus corrected-engine semantics. Decade config ≠ release_v1 config (the 2025 sanity check is directional: deaths agree within 0.05–3.1%). weather_2025.json was re-fetched 2026-07-31 with the same pinned-ERA5 URL but a different api_response_sha256 — ERA5 revision drift possible.
- **F7:** single seed, no CIs — the "across seeds" gate condition cannot even be evaluated. Cross-engine (Python mesh_sim vs Rust fastsim, parity-checked but not identical) and cross-scale (312 nodes/49 renters, hop_limit 3 vs 457/212 — the documented `--kiosk-demand-scale 0.25` deviation); survival conclusions look solid (identical 159 unique-nodes-died on full-listen arms) but PDR magnitudes are not apples-to-apples. No matched Python-engine non-learned baselines exist anywhere in artifacts. RL runs are absent from the reearn manifest (no hash/engine-head record). routing-algorithms.md's gates are prospective text, not a formal preregistration.

**Bottom line.** Four claim families survive contact with the corrected engine essentially intact (1, 2, 3, and the single-gateway core of 5), two re-earn their headline direction while shedding sub-claims that must not be re-quoted (4: "3× headroom" and "SOS survived everywhere"; 6: duty's PDR/SOS parity), and the RL victories (7) are confirmed dead — now a documented negative result rather than a withdrawn positive one.
