# Audit and correction ledger — 2026-07-13

This ledger records the project's evidence status after the July 2026 whole-repository
audit. It is the durable index; the older design notes and generated reports are not
duplicated here.

**Bottom line:** this is a substantial research prototype, but it is not yet a validated,
legally cleared emergency network. The audit found false, mislabelled, and unsupported
statements, including claims presented too confidently. It found no evidence from the
repository of deliberate deception. Intent cannot be inferred from a bad claim, so this
ledger classifies defects and evidence, not whether anyone “lied.”

Severity means: **critical** can reverse a central scientific, safety, coverage, or legal
conclusion; **high** materially affects reproducibility or interpretation; **medium** is a
bounded ambiguity or metadata defect. “Fixed” means corrected in the present worktree,
not necessarily merged, released, field-validated, or used for a replacement full-scale
run.
